Five-Stage Architecture Thinking

  1. Identify the inherited assumption. Which work is treated as necessary, which implementation as the only possible expression, and which component metric as user value?
  2. Identify the changing conditions. Which shift in physical cost, parallel mapping, power constraints, or system scale makes the old assumption begin to fail?
  3. Establish the causal mechanism. Specify where costs are eliminated, shifted, or hidden. Hiding a wait does not eliminate the underlying latency.
  4. Test the crossover condition. Account for both savings and new overhead. Fix the workload, quality, and comparison boundary, and explain where the conclusion reverses.
  5. Define the architectural action. Decide whether policy belongs in representation, compilation, scheduling, interfaces, or the complete system. Name a diagnostic that could falsify the hypothesis.
