What changes the architecture decision

Procure a commissioned system, not a capex headline.

Supply assurances refer to different stages: component allocation, equipment delivery, powered-shell completion, utility agreements and service acceptance. CoreWeave identifies shell execution in one delayed project; Equinix identifies power in dense facilities. Those statements concern different constraints and need not conflict.

Decision implication: Require a dated chain of commitments from wafers and memory through integration, energization and workload acceptance. Record the owner and recovery plan at each stage.

The CPU question is now part of AI capacity planning.

Compute vendors increasingly describe orchestration and tool execution alongside accelerator work. HBM requirements are rising, while suppliers discuss conventional DRAM, flash, cache offload and stronger agreements. Additional HBM output competes for manufacturing resources with other products. Statements about samples, booked capacity and shipments represent different evidence levels.

Method, denominators and evidence limits

The original inventory contained 35 × 4 = 140 quarterly slots, all with transcript sources and selected Q&A coverage; the 5 October refresh adds one quarterly event (Micron FY2026 Q4) and one post-earnings call, for 141 quarterly events + 1 post-earnings call. Retrieval and keyword-assisted candidate identification were followed by editorial selection and answer review. The 178 probes are grouped question-level records, with related subparts retained where the answer addresses them together; they are not 178 exhaustively normalized atomic asks. Compute suppliers receive more selected probes than most other companies. Repeated themes retain separate company-quarter provenance and are synthesized above rather than erased.

Source hierarchy: company-hosted material was used where available, supplemented by full transcript mirrors of the actual management exchange. Media-call and prerecorded ASML entries were replaced with analyst calls; a mislabeled Lam Q4 entry and an inconsistent Applied Q1 entry were replaced. Original audio was not systematically replayed. There is no separate online audience-question corpus to reconcile, and absence from the selected ledger must not be interpreted as silence on the call. Translation, speaker-label and transcription errors remain possible; ambiguous attribution is marked. Numerical performance claims and apparent unit errors were not promoted into independently verified specifications.

Coding definitions

Answered: directly addresses the selected substantive ask, even if qualitative.
Partially answered: provides relevant information but leaves a requested component unresolved.
Deferred: explicitly moves disclosure to a later occasion.
Deflected: substitutes an adjacent issue without addressing the ask; no selected record was assigned this label.
Unanswered: the requested disclosure was not provided in the reviewed exchange, including an explicit refusal to quantify.

A follow-up can be a missing part of the original answer or a new validation requirement proposed here. It does not automatically make a direct answer “partial.” Status applies at that call date; later quarters do not retroactively change it.
