Patterns in the reviewed Q&A

Feed the accelerator before judging arithmetic.

Samsung attributed the gap between PIM bandwidth uplift and application speedup to unchanged host and ordering overheads. Facebook emphasized ingestion benchmarking and reuse. Translation, DRAM-bank behavior and preprocessing still limited useful throughput. S4 88:51 ↗

The software path is part of the architecture.

Skydio described expensive architecture-specific optimization during porting. Alder Lake Linux-support questions survived without recoverable answers. Facebook's merged reads were client software. An engine or instruction did not establish a ready application path. K2 47:07 ↗

Transport and ordering define integration.

IBM described scoped ordering around Telum's accelerator; Enflame used noncoherent copies and mailboxes. Mount Evans' transport details were deferred. Compatibility, coherence and reliable transport were distinct properties requiring separate evidence. S1 97:29 ↗

Precision needs replay and quality evidence.

Graphcore described controlled seeds for stochastic-rounding replay. Xilinx distinguished BF16 from block floating point; Anton selected formats by pipeline stage. Efficiency depended on numerical behavior and workload quality rather than the format name alone. S6 29:24 ↗

Density and power need separate proof.

Intel discussed PowerVia with stacking and EMIB's yield/reach tradeoffs. Anton justified a global clock mesh through latency and power. TSMC cooling questions lacked useful caption answers; package scalability did not establish facility feasibility. T2.1 52:49 ↗

Implications and concrete vendor asks

Analyst inference: these present-day acceptance tests follow from historical uncertainties. Historical statements and roadmap promises do not establish later delivery.

Decision and timingVendor ask and decision test
Specialized compute Before selectionRun matched end-to-end workloads including ingestion, host orchestration and storage. Measure utilization, tails and whole-system energy against a CPU baseline; profile the remaining bottleneck.
Software migration Before resource commitmentRequest versioned compiler/runtime/kernel support and a reproducible port. Measure engineering effort, rejected operators and performance against the deployment schedule.
Accelerator/DPU integration Before host commitmentSpecify coherence, ordering, transport and management separately. Test contended transfers, mixed hosts and recovery with versioned software and correctness checks.
Reduced precision Before model approvalCompare quality and convergence across formats and seeds. Verify deterministic replay; identify operations requiring higher precision and include their overhead.
Package and cooling Before platform commitmentRequest design-kit access, process/yield evidence and steady/transient power. Test expected inlet temperature and flow, including facility energy and service limits.
