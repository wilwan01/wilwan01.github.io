Counting rules and comparison limits

One independently answerable technical ask, one primary theme. Moderator prompts, unclear origins and unintelligible asks are excluded from the new historical heatmaps. Missing captions are absent evidence, never zero. Tutorials and keynotes are included in 2017–2021; later source populations and question opportunities differ. No new Slack content was accessed.

Share = primary-theme asks ÷ all eligible audience asks in that year. These twelve shared themes omit other primary families, so displayed shares do not sum to100%. Rows rank by mean within-year share, giving each year equal weight. Exact residual counts and source profiles appear in the data download. Different seats. Better questions.

Presenters & sell-side architects

Make the trade-off visible. Show the workload, baseline, system boundary and conditions under which the design wins.

Organisers

Connect sessions around shared bottlenecks. Pair component innovation with deployment evidence and preserve the unanswered questions.

Buy-side architects & hyperscalers

Turn each disclosure into an evaluation question: what changes in fleet efficiency, service latency or deployment risk?

Implementors

Look for interface costs, software assumptions, verification burden and failure behaviour behind the block diagram.

Investors & analysts

Separate architectural promise from adoption evidence. Track deployment readiness, ecosystem dependencies and customer qualification.

Attendees

Read across sessions. The most consequential pattern may be the same bottleneck appearing in very different products.
