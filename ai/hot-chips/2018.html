Patterns in the reviewed Q&A

Security needs an explicit contract.

The Spectre/Meltdown panel discussed protected execution subcontexts and shared accelerator state. Cascade Lake supplied no verification evidence for mitigation coverage. Titan narrowed the trusted surface without removing every CPU, software or foundry dependency. K1 88:26 ↗

Programmability shifts work into mapping.

Project Everest disclosed no autovectorizing compiler and deferred receive-programming details. Pixel Visual Core and dynamic-graph questions tested what developers still had to map and synchronize. Higher-level access did not prove transparent application portability. S5 90:37 ↗

Memory needs lifecycle accounting.

Mythic's intermediate activations occupied tile SRAM; A64FX had an HBM capacity ceiling. Optane answers withheld requested endurance and operating-state power figures. Density and bandwidth alone did not establish service lifetime or working-set fit. S9 123:48 ↗

Sparsity benefits differ by mechanism.

Arm said 3D convolution could be mapped without native support; DeePhi used structured sparsity. Mythic said zero weights would reduce current without increasing dense-matrix capacity. Energy, compute and capacity benefits required workload-specific evidence. S6 64:46 ↗

Efficiency claims need matching boundaries.

Samsung's power comparison was not fully process-normalized. XDNN described mapped compute rather than all FPGA resources. NRAM cell energy and Mythic whole-chip energy covered different budgets; their ratios could not be compared directly. S1 38:28 ↗

Implications and concrete vendor asks

Analyst inference: these present-day acceptance tests follow from historical uncertainties. Historical statements and roadmap promises do not establish later delivery.

Decision and timingVendor ask and decision test
Shared-compute security Before deploymentRequest protected-state and mitigation-validation coverage. Test cache, predictor and device-state leakage across hostile contexts; measure the cost of required isolation.
Application portability During proof of conceptRequest compiler coverage, partitioning and synchronization rules. Port dynamic and unfamiliar operators; count manual work and fallback overhead at the service latency target.
Capacity and persistence Before platform commitmentDisclose SRAM limits, endurance, encryption location and idle/read/write power. Sweep working sets and sustained writes against the service lifetime and complete energy budget.
Numeric/operator fit Before accelerator selectionSpecify native/mapped operators and which sparsity patterns save compute, energy or capacity. Test target shapes and precision settings at fixed model quality.
Comparative efficiency Before procurement rankingRequest matching baselines, process assumptions and resource occupancy. Reproduce useful throughput and energy including host, memory, interfaces and co-resident workloads.
