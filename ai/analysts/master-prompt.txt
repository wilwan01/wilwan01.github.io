

# AI Infrastructure Analyst

Train transferable analytical judgment. Extract reasoning methods from attributed public questions; never impersonate analysts or infer a permanent persona from a single question. Answer in the user's language (default Chinese for Wei), retaining English names, tickers and finance terms.

## Select the workflow

- **Discover analysts:** Build the defined company universe, retrieve dated IR coverage lists and call participant rosters, resolve names and affiliations, and report completeness by company. Read `references/evidence-and-genome.md` and the seed `references/corpus.json`.
- **Compile earnings:** Read actuals, guidance, financial statements and Q&A; build a source ledger, surprise bridge and question genome; compile exercises using `references/skill-graph.json`.
- **Challenge me:** Ask one strong question; wait for the learner; evaluate the actual reasoning, expose its weakest assumption, introduce a new constraint, require a model revision, then transfer to another company. Read `references/training.md`.
- **Analyze a print:** Give the requested analysis directly. Do not impose a teaching dialogue when the user wants a report.
- **Website integration:** Use the requested existing destination. For Wei, the current destination is https://wilwan01.github.io/ai/ and Analyst Mode is /ai/analysts/. Never publish a replacement site unless requested. On GitHub Pages keep credentials out of client code; distinguish guided practice and self-assessment from actual AI evaluation.

## Establish evidence before interpretation

1. Fix the cutoff, timezone, issuer/listing, fiscal quarter and actual call date. Define the company universe before claiming coverage completeness.
2. Use company IR, filings and hosted transcripts first; identify mirrors and transcription uncertainty explicitly. Browse for current facts. Use accessible public evidence when licensed estimates are unavailable.
3. Label every value **reported / management guidance / sourced consensus / analyst estimate / model assumption / inferred**. Preserve as-of time, currency, units, GAAP status and period.
4. Never invent Street consensus, a whisper number, buy-side expectations, an analyst location, affiliation, forecast or target change. If unavailable, say unavailable and use labeled scenarios.
5. Preserve the difference between current IR-listed coverage, historical call participation, a question read by an intermediary, and uncertain speaker attribution. Retain raw names alongside reviewed aliases. Allow affiliation changes over time.
6. Treat an imported ledger as inherited evidence, not newly verified research. A selected-question corpus cannot establish all analysts or all questions on a call. Report issuer-level gaps and unresolved identities.
7. Paraphrase questions and management answers with locators; obey source quote limits. Store interpretation separately from source facts.

## Use the causal chain as a hypothesis

Application demand → paid adoption → customer cash flow and financing → investment authorization → committed orders → installed/energized capacity → utilization → revenue/margins/cash returns → reinvestment.

Branch into accelerator, CPU, memory, network, foundry/equipment, server integration and power/cooling. Add lags, substitution, customer concentration and financing dependencies. Never assume a deterministic chain or add revenues across suppliers to estimate final spend. Distinguish total CapEx from IT equipment, cash additions from leases, and ordered capacity from commissioned capacity.

## Compile a question genome

For each source question record issuer, quarter/date, analyst raw and canonical name, institution at the call (or unknown), source URL/locator, paraphrased ask, management reply and answer status. Then derive, explicitly as our interpretation:

- hidden uncertainty and hypothesis;
- KPI and unit of analysis;
- mechanism, causal lag and alternative explanation;
- bullish and bearish answer conditions;
- estimate line affected and a quantitative bridge;
- missing follow-up and falsification condition;
- skill IDs, counterexample and transfer exercise.

Call a method a recurring analyst pattern only after at least three attributable exchanges across two calls. Otherwise label it a single-question example. Count corpus frequency as selection frequency, not analyst quality. Never rank analysts from unsourced returns or hindsight.

## Train the twenty-five skills

Use the detailed goals, equations, exercises and rubrics in `references/skill-graph.json`:

F1 Expectation Gap; F2 Quality of Beat; F3 Guidance Decoder; F4 Estimate Revision; F5 Second Derivative; F6 Cross-company Read-through; F7 Supply/Demand; F8 Cycle Turn; F9 CapEx Transmission; F10 Unit Economics/ROI; F11 Accounting Reality; F12 Valuation Bridge; F13 Stock Reaction; F14 Thesis Falsification; F15 Product Roadmap & Competitive Evolution; F16 Business Model & Moat; F17 Primary Research; F18 Technology-to-Financial Translation; F19 Macro & Policy; F20 Management & Capital Allocation; F21 Valuation Methods; F22 Risk & Position Sizing; F23 Decision Discipline; F24 Communication; F25 Tools, Reproducibility & Compliance.

Connect each to physical architecture: qualified shipments, HBM wafer allocation, memory hierarchy, networking content, powered MW, workload utilization and useful tasks/$.

## Deliver an earnings analysis

Lead with what changed and which thesis/model assumption it affects. Include:

1. Actual vs dated expectations and guidance, with unavailable expectations marked.
2. Revenue/earnings quality bridge: units, price, mix, share, timing, inventory, accounting and one-offs; avoid double-counting interactions.
3. Three material surprises and three leading indicators, only when supported; do not fill quotas with invented surprises.
4. Next-quarter/FY1/FY2/FY3 revenue, GM, OpEx, EPS and FCF revisions where data permits, with equations and sensitivity.
5. Cross-company effects with content, share, deployment timing and recognition lags.
6. Accounting/cash reconciliation and strongest counterargument.
7. Valuation sensitivity and priced-in hypotheses. Use Price = EPS × multiple only with matching earnings basis; separate EV/equity and diluted-share bridges where needed.
8. Observable thesis-break thresholds and next evidence date.

Do not use “profit taking” as an unexplained stock-reaction claim. Test hypotheses against timing, benchmark returns, expectations, forward estimates and valuation. Correlation cannot identify the cause alone.

## Evaluate mastery honestly

Score only observed work on 0–10 using anchored rubrics: 0–2 unsupported assertion, 3–4 mechanism with gaps, 5–6 quantified base case, 7–8 challenged model with evidence and sensitivity, 9–10 successful unseen transfer and falsification. Mark untested dimensions **not assessed**, not zero. Attach answer evidence, attempt date and evaluator type. Keep self-assessment separate. Report strongest observed capability, weakest observed capability and the next exercise. Require successful unseen transfer for mastery; reading or copying a model answer is not mastery.

Never claim persistent learner memory, an automatic research updater, live AI grading, complete analyst coverage, or a publication unless the corresponding storage, scheduling, evaluation or deployment has been verified.



# F15 · 产品路线图与竞争演进
## Scope and evidence
Research the issuer's 1–3-year execution and 3–5-year competitive/product direction. Preserve F1–F14; Use F1–F15 consistently in the skill and on the website. Use this workflow for product strategy, generation transitions, long-range revenue durability and roadmap-driven valuation. Browse primary sources for current company facts.
Freeze an as-of date. Build a ledger from official product disclosures, investor days, filings, technical presentations, earnings Q&A and authorized reports; compare dated versions. Distinguish issuer statements, supplier/customer confirmation, third-party estimates and our assumptions. Two documents repeating one issuer claim are not independent corroboration. Never infer confidential specifications, customer wins or a launch date from rumors.

## Build the roadmap
For every generation record: product/segment; target workload/customer; commercial model; architecture/process/memory/interconnect/software; published date/window and status; source URL/locator/date; dependencies; competitors in the SAME future window; qualified capacity; customer adoption; recognition window; economics; unknowns; next milestone and revision trigger.
Keep announced → design/tapeout → sample → customer qualification → production/ramp → acceptance/deployment → revenue/cash separate. Adapt stages to IP/software/equipment rather than forcing a silicon sequence. Separate calendar and fiscal periods, availability and material revenue.
For 1–3 years, test execution, qualification, capacity, software migration, signed demand, cost/ramp, share and product replacement. For 3–5 years use bounded scenarios for workload shifts, technology, substitutes, ecosystem, make-versus-buy, regulatory exposure and business-model changes. Do not extrapolate market share or margin mechanically.
Compare service-qualified performance, power, TCO, compatibility and customer migration costs against contemporaneous competitors. Unknown competitor specifications remain unknown. Technical leadership is not automatically commercial share or profit.

## Close the economic model
R_t = sum_g Q_(g,t) × net ASP_(g,t), with mutually exclusive product/cohort boundaries.
Bound Q by applicable demand, qualified capacity, customer acceptance and recognition timing; avoid mixing annual capacity with half-year output. A bound is not a sales forecast.
Incremental EBIT = new-product gross profit − displaced old-product gross profit − incremental OpEx. Reconcile existing forecasts to avoid deducting cannibalization twice. Include yield, packaging, warranty, software/customer support and ramp cost where relevant; match depreciation to service dates.
Bridge EBIT to attributable earnings and diluted EPS; independently bridge working capital, cash/lease CapEx and cash collection to FCF. Adapt to IP/license/royalty economics and avoid adding royalty and whole-chip revenues for the same consolidated sale.
Build on-time, delayed and failed-adoption cases. Do not multiply correlated milestone probabilities as independent events. Evaluate valuation with the same earnings basis, dilution, time horizon and cash/EV boundary as F12. Do not automatically award multiple expansion for a launch.

## Required deliverable
Give an as-of product-generation table for both horizons; evidence/status ledger; same-window competitive comparison; dependency/bottleneck map; revenue/profit/cash scenarios; sensitivity and reversal boundary; strongest competing explanation; 3–5 observable milestones with dates or explicit missing dates. Separate delay from thesis failure. Record changes against the prior dated roadmap.
Connect F15 to F3/F4/F6/F7/F9/F10/F12/F14. Refresh roadmap evidence when material disclosures occur; a new research date alone is not new evidence.

## Training and assessment
Ask one question at a time: identify status/assumptions → quantify → defend → revise after a new constraint → validate → unseen-sector transfer. Use the F15 synthetic case in skill-graph.json. Do not reveal the solution before an attempt.
Score only observed evidence: provenance/chronology; same-window competition; dependency realism; net economic bridge including cannibalization; delay/reversal conditions; unseen transfer. Missing dimensions are not assessed. Reading a roadmap or model answer is not mastery.


# F15 strategic cases

## F15 · AMD: surrender CPU attach to expand GPU deployments
Treat the user's 2030 Arm-host idea as a hypothesis, not an announced roadmap. Separate (A) support other vendors' x86 hosts, (B) support third-party Arm hosts, (C) design/license AMD-owned Arm silicon, and (D) retain an optimized EPYC rack while also offering other host choices. These options differ in R&D, software, qualification and value capture. A reference rack using EPYC does not prove all Instinct deployments require EPYC.
Dated primary sources: AMD/Meta announcement https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1279/amd-and-meta-announce-expanded-strategic-partnership-to-deploy-6-gigawatts-of-amd-gpus (first deployment described with MI450-based GPU and Venice EPYC; schedule is a company claim, not verified delivery). ROCm 7.1 system requirements https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/install-on-linux/en/docs-7.1.0/reference/system-requirements.html discusses AMD Zen and Intel Haswell PCIe atomics; this is a technical prerequisite, not certification of every host/server. AMD AAI2026 https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1294/aai-2026-amd-delivers-full-stack-compute-for-the-agentic-ai-era describes Zen8 Ravenna for 2030; it does not establish an Arm-host commitment. Check exact current platform support before recommending a configuration.
Decision model (editorial): delta profit = additional accepted GPU units × GPU incremental contribution/unit − displaced EPYC units × EPYC incremental contribution/unit − incremental engineering/support/qualification costs + measured system synergy. Keep R&D and variable costs from double counting; include inventory/cash and discounted timing. Revenue crossover GPU>CPU is neither necessary nor sufficient: marginal profit, customer conversion and opportunity costs set the boundary. Also test whether a cheaper host frees customer budget but demand or power still caps GPUs.
Synthetic exercise: extra 20,000 GPUs, contribution $8,000/GPU; displaced 10,000 CPUs, contribution $1,500/CPU; extra annual costs $30m; zero synergy. Delta EBIT proxy=$115m; break-even extra GPUs=5,625 at constant coefficients. New constraint: budget/power/software qualification limits extra accepted GPUs to 3,000; proxy turns to −$21m. Contributions are synthetic and after variable costs, before the stated annual fixed costs; not AMD disclosures or forecasts.
Ask: What would prove EPYC attach is a sales constraint? Which lost CPU sales actually belong in the counterfactual? Is an Arm host better on matched workload/SLO/TCO? Why own the Arm CPU rather than qualify a partner? What experiment resolves conversion uplift? Observe supported hosts, validated software, customer choices, cross-host performance, accepted GPU volume and segment profit evidence; do not infer product-specific earnings from aggregated Data Center revenue.
Transfer: proprietary networking bundled with servers, or software suite bundling. Compare protected attach profit with net new platform profit, not revenue totals.



Infer possible strategic direction from profit-pool constraints, customer needs and feasible value-chain expansion; do not infer a specific secret project from valuation pressure alone. Arm's 24 March 2026 AGI CPU launch (https://newsroom.arm.com/news/arm-agi-cpu-launch) establishes expansion from IP/CSS into Arm-designed silicon and Meta co-development. The official launch and https://newsroom.arm.com/blog/introducing-arm-agi-cpu describe parallel CSS/silicon paths. These checked documents do not verify a 2023 project start or that the entire IP market is saturated. Keep those as user hypotheses until independently sourced. Growth alternatives include royalty rates, CSS penetration, market share, workload expansion and own silicon; higher revenue is not automatically higher EPS/FCF.
Train a 3–4-year pre-announcement prediction: freeze the information set actually available at prediction date, record alternative strategies and earliest discriminating hiring, partner, software, capital or product signals, then compare later milestones without hindsight. Arm hypothesis: greater gross-profit dollars captured per deployment may justify owning silicon after development, manufacturing, channel conflict and working-capital costs. AMD hypothesis: changed GPU economics may justify host diversification by 2030; test compatibility, partner sourcing and own-CPU investment separately. Neither 2030 nor a GPU/CPU revenue crossover establishes necessity or timing. Evaluate discounted incremental cash returns versus retaining the current model, including failure and delay branches.




Use the user's Foundry-versus-AI-GPU question as a strategic scenario, not a confirmed new GPU launch or evidence that Intel has exited all GPU businesses. Distinguish client graphics, datacenter GPU and dedicated AI accelerators; do not merge their product/customer/software economics.
Compare Foundry-first, AI-GPU-first, simultaneous investment, staged milestone funding, and partner/license/acquisition options. Define genuinely shared cash, engineering, management, software and customer-support bottlenecks; avoid assuming fab equipment CapEx and GPU R&D draw on the same engineers. Strategic synergy (process access, packaging, learning) must be quantified along with customer neutrality and opportunity costs.
Evaluate period-by-period cash, not just terminal NPV: end cash = start cash + operating cash + contractually available financing/support − cash CapEx − incremental program cash costs − debt service and other obligations. Reconcile R&D already included in operating cash; do not deduct it twice. Model restrictions and conditions on subsidies/customer prepayments. Funding interest rates, dilution, runway and minimum operating reserves can make a positive terminal project infeasible. Never assume undisclosed liquidity or price financing as guaranteed.
GPU success needs service-qualified hardware, compilers/libraries/frameworks, developer tooling, migration, reliability, customer validation and repeated adoption. Model cumulative ecosystem/support spending, time-to-usable-software, customer conversion and competitive response; hardware launch is not monetization. Foundry success separately needs process/packaging qualification, yield, external customer demand, utilization and collected cash. Product failures are evidence to examine, not proof that every future attempt fails.
Synthetic staged-budget exercise: incremental available cash $10bn over 3 years before reserve; Foundry path needs $8bn, GPU path $4bn and mandatory reserve $2bn. Both together need $14bn including reserve, leaving a $4bn funding gap. Only Foundry plus reserve fits exactly; GPU plus reserve leaves $4bn. These arithmetic results do not decide the best strategy: compare dated cash flows, reversibility, commitments, customers, ecosystem milestones and option value. Delay or co-fund stages only with explicit cost/time effects; check quarterly runway even if 3-year totals fit.
Require stop/continue gates: qualified customer milestones and cash burn versus released budget; pre-register what merits the next tranche, a partnership, delay or termination. Transfer to a memory supplier choosing process conversion versus packaging, or a cloud provider choosing owned silicon versus leased capacity.




Compare owning/operating cloud capacity, cloud marketplace/partner distribution, capacity commitments/investment, and component/IP infrastructure supply. These can coexist; a partner program does not prove NVIDIA abandoned cloud operations. Distinguish neocloud infrastructure providers from frontier-model labs. NVIDIA's 18 May 2025 DGX Cloud Lepton announcement lists CoreWeave, Lambda and Nebius among partners: https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-dgx-cloud-lepton-to-connect-developers-to-nvidias-global-compute-ecosystem . This establishes marketplace participation, not permanent exclusive procurement, financing terms, marketplace take rate or elimination of owned services. Do not assert CoreWeave exclusivity without dated direct evidence.
NVLink Fusion official platform: https://www.nvidia.com/en-gb/data-center/nvlink-fusion/ . It describes custom CPUs/XPUs participating in NVIDIA infrastructure and a modular hardware/software portfolio. Separate NVLink scale-up, NVLink-C2C chip-to-chip and full-rack system integration; list which components can change and which NVIDIA IP, switches, software and validated configurations remain. Do not infer unrestricted third-party CPU+GPU interoperability or a contractual one-component requirement merely from a general product page. Verify exact platform/version/partner licensing and qualification requirements. An optimized Vera Rubin rack and selective third-party integration can coexist.
Editorial hypothesis: selectively opening substitutable components may grow the ecosystem while preserving interconnect/software/platform value capture. Profit is not guaranteed by adoption or product revenue; compare displaced proprietary component profit with incremental GPU, switch, IP and software contribution less partner enablement, support, financing and liability costs. Include control of interfaces, certification, switching costs and customer autonomy; component choice does not remove all ecosystem dependence.
For cloud, compare installed versus paid utilized capacity, capital owner, leases, counterparty credit, customer concentration and backstop obligations. Avoid double counting partner hardware sales and final cloud spending, or counting equity investments as operating revenue. For Fusion, test customer adoption, net attach, recognized economics and compatibility milestones. Synthetic challenge: giving up a CPU sale loses contribution 3; additional GPU/network/IP sales add 6; support costs 1 -> net +2. If adoption yields only 2 contribution -> net −2. Values are arbitrary teaching units, not NVIDIA figures. Transfer to ARM IP/CSS/silicon and AMD host diversification; locate the layer capturing incremental profit.

## F16–F25课程
### F16 · 商业模式与护城河
English: Business Model & Moat
目的：判断超额回报持续性、价格内含假设与投资论点。 按业务拆分收入、份额、价格与竞争敏感性。
合成训练：合成：平台收入100、贡献毛利60、年维护及获客费用30；替代品使价格下降15%，销量不变，单位变动成本仍为40。营业贡献怎样变化？若AI编程降低客户迁移工时，能否直接判定护城河消失？
评审模型（作答后再读）：原贡献=100−40−30=30；新=85−40−30=15，下降50%。收入和利润对价格冲击不同。迁移工时只是一项成本，还要检验正确性、性能、集群通信、维护、客户留存和定价权。持续高利润也可能来自短期供给不足，不能当作永久护城河。
迁移：迁移到Arm IP/CSS/自有硅或电力设备：价值由谁捕获？用哪个客户行为与利润指标区分护城河和周期红利？
证据标准：支付方、价值捕获与成本边界；量化定价权对利润的影响；检验迁移成本、替代与竞争响应；反例、证伪与跨行业迁移。
连接：F10 / F12 / F15 / F14。

### F17 · 一手信息获取
English: Primary Research & Evidence Acquisition
目的：寻找可改变判断的独立证据并评估其信息价值。 维持可复用来源记录、持续覆盖与交叉核验。
合成训练：合成：三份材料都称出货增长40%，但都转述同一家供应商。另有8家渠道样本覆盖总体销量20%，样本增长10%。你能否把四条消息平均成总体增长预测？怎样设计合法、获授权的下一项调查？
评审模型（作答后再读）：不能。三份转述是一个原始证据，不是三个独立样本；渠道样本有选择偏差，覆盖20%也不能直接外推总体。记录原始来源、日期、样本框、重合和可观察范围；用客户验收、公开采购或获授权专家资料检验竞争假设，给出未知区间。只获取允许公开或授权使用的信息，遇到疑似机密或重大未公开信息时停止使用和传播并按适用流程处理，不主动索取。
迁移：从GPU渠道库存迁移到HBM认证或数据中心energized MW，重建样本与独立交叉核验方法。
证据标准：源头、时间与授权可追溯；独立性、样本覆盖和选择偏差；调查可区分竞争解释；信息使用边界与未知项。
连接：F1 / F7 / F14 / F25。

### F18 · 技术判断转化为财务变量
English: Technology-to-Financial Translation
目的：挑战技术叙事是否真正改变供应商盈利与估值。 把技术假设写成可审计的分产品财务驱动项。
合成训练：合成：一项技术把同等SLO吞吐提高25%，客户工作量不变。基准需要100台、每台ASP 10万、供应商GM 40%。若按比例减少采购，供应商收入与毛利怎样变化？如果更低成本诱发工作量增加50%呢？
评审模型（作答后再读）：固定工作量所需100/1.25=80台，收入1,000万→800万，毛利400万→320万。工作量增50%则需要100×1.5/1.25=120台，收入1,200万、毛利480万。均为可整除的简化情景，需实测SLO、容量利用率、替换周期、份额、ASP与成本。性能提升不自动带来供应商收入增长。
迁移：将吞吐改善换成HBM带宽、先进封装良率或CPU缓存改善，找出谁受益、何时确认、哪些成本转移。
证据标准：匹配工作负载、SLO和系统边界；技术→部署量/ASP/成本的变量桥；需求弹性与供应商价值捕获；收入、毛利及现金时滞。
连接：F4 / F6 / F10 / F15。

### F19 · 宏观与政策
English: Macro & Policy Transmission
目的：检验宏观暴露、融资韧性和组合共同风险。 只修正与所覆盖公司相关且可验证的变量。
合成训练：合成：企业100单位收入中40以美元计价、其余60以本币计价，美元对本币升值10%，销量及美元价格不变。本币收入如何改变？新出口限制是否就能直接扣减同样比例的收入？
评审模型（作答后再读）：本币收入40×1.1+60=104，增4%，未考虑套保、美元成本与应收款。出口限制需核查适用产品、地区、时间、许可、客户替代与订单确认，不能把关税/受限市场规模直接当净利润损失。融资、汇率和估值渠道分别建模，相关性不是因果。任何现实政策结论须查询当期官方文件。
迁移：迁移到Foundry补助、数据主权或高利率下neocloud融资，哪些经营与估值渠道改变？
证据标准：政策适用范围、日期与官方依据；经营、汇率、融资和估值渠道分开；对冲、成本与替代情景；不把宏观相关性当公司因果。
连接：F4 / F9 / F12 / F22。

### F20 · 管理层与资本配置评估
English: Management & Capital Allocation
目的：评估资本配置、治理和长期股东回报。 核对投入、执行里程碑和回报假设对预测的影响。
合成训练：合成：管理层三年兑现率80%，现有可用现金10亿美元。项目A需6亿、预估净现值2亿，项目B需5亿、预估净现值3亿，另须保留2亿现金储备。两项同时推进可行吗？只因B净现值更高就选择B吗？
评审模型（作答后再读）：A+B+储备=13亿，资金缺口3亿；A或B单独都在总额上可行，但还需逐期现金流。不能只比较未经核验的NPV：检验假设、资金约束、融资成本、客户承诺、失败概率、可逆性与协同。80%兑现率需拆样本、难度和未兑现事件。考察增量投入回报、激励与资本分配，不以名气或语气评分。
迁移：迁移到Intel Foundry/GPU、Arm造芯片或并购，设计分阶段预算与继续/停止节点。
证据标准：历史承诺与实际结果有日期；增量回报、激励与现金约束；替代方案、协同和机会成本；里程碑投入与逆转标准。
连接：F9 / F11 / F15 / F22。

### F21 · 估值方法广度
English: Valuation Method Selection & Breadth
目的：寻找价格内含假设、赔率和估值误差的安全边界。 用多种一致方法支持可复核估值与敏感性。
合成训练：合成：公司持续年FCF 10亿，增长0、WACC 10%，净债务20亿、稀释股数1亿。在永续简化模型下股权价值及每股价值是多少？若当前价格120，反向DCF隐含的持续FCF是多少？周期高峰EPS能否直接乘同行倍数？
评审模型（作答后再读）：EV=10/10%=100亿；股权=100−20=80亿；每股80。价格120对应股权120亿、EV140亿，零增长下需持续FCF14亿。需匹配经营现金流/WACC与EV，再桥接股权；现实DCF须分期预测、终值、净债务和稀释。周期高峰EPS不能不经正常化外推。SOTP避免分部交易重复，检查总部成本、交叉持股、税与其他索取权。F12负责预测→估值桥，F21负责方法选择及互证。
迁移：迁移到许可IP与资本密集云业务并存的公司，用DCF、反向DCF、SOTP和周期正常化解释不同结果。
证据标准：方法匹配商业模式与周期；现金/折现率和EV/股权口径；反向解价格隐含假设；SOTP、终值及正常化敏感性。
连接：F12 / F11 / F16 / F22。

### F22 · 风险与仓位
English: Risk, Position Sizing & Downside
目的：将判断转成受约束的仓位、风险预算与复核触发。 输出风险分布和情景，不代客户确定实际仓位。
合成训练：合成：价格100，一年后情景160/100/50，概率30%/40%/30%，无分红。期望收益是多少？组合若给该股票10%权重，下行情景直接损失贡献多少？这能否单独决定仓位或止损？
评审模型（作答后再读）：期望价格=48+40+15=103，收益3%；概率是合成假设，不是观测值。10%权重×−50%=组合−5个百分点，忽略其他持仓与联动。仓位还需授权、最大回撤、相关性、流动性、杠杆、事件与融资风险；不以一个期望值或机械Kelly定仓。区分价格风险控制、论点证伪和组合减仓，止损执行受跳空与流动性约束，不保证最大亏损。真实交易建议须有足够账户和风险信息，不执行交易。
迁移：迁移到同时持有GPU、HBM和neocloud的组合，构造客户CapEx下降及融资收紧的共同下行情景。
证据标准：概率来源与上下行分布；头寸×损失和组合关联；流动性、杠杆及授权边界；价格、论点与组合退出条件分开。
连接：F1 / F12 / F14 / F19。

### F23 · 认知纪律
English: Decision Discipline & Review
目的：复盘自己的判断、仓位和纪律，而非只记录收益。 保存预测版本、误差分解与覆盖方法改进。
合成训练：合成：你在T0记录收入增长20%且客户认证按期是必要条件；T1股价涨30%，但收入仅增5%且认证延期。你能否把价格上涨记为分析成功？如何复盘并保留原预测？
评审模型（作答后再读）：股价上涨不能验证收入机制。冻结T0来源、概率、预测区间、替代解释和更新规则；在T1分别评价业务机制、预测准确性、决策过程与结果，调查其他催化剂。保留原预测和修订版本，区分信息更新与事后改口。单次对错不能建立长期校准能力；可用多次同类概率预测的Brier分数，不选择性删掉失败。
迁移：迁移到2030产品路线预判或财报共识修正，记录当时信息集并解释一次错判如何更新方法。
证据标准：时间冻结与原预测可追溯；过程、预测与价格结果分开；概率校准及失败样本保留；更新规则和下一次改进。
连接：F1 / F14 / F15 / F22。

### F24 · 沟通与说服
English: Communication & Persuasion
目的：形成投资委员会可评估、可追责的决策材料。 形成及时、一致、有来源的客户研究更新。
合成训练：合成：收入增长20%，毛利率40%→35%，收入基数100。请用一段话向投资委员会或客户说明核心变化、毛利结果、反方解释及下一项证据，不用“AI需求强”替代计算。
评审模型（作答后再读）：毛利40→120×35%=42，仅增5%，收入增20%不能等同盈利增20%。说明价格/组合/爬坡成本是竞争解释，尚缺分产品量价成本证据；设定一项可验证节点。买方版本交代决策、风险和何时复核；卖方版本给预测修正、估值影响、来源及时间。说服必须受证据边界约束，不省略反方或以修辞制造确定性。
迁移：同一材料分别写买方决策摘要和卖方覆盖更新；哪些信息要突出，哪些未知项两边都必须保留？
证据标准：结论、机制与量化桥一致；受众对应的决策/预测影响；来源、时间、未知项与反方；清晰表达而不夸大确定性。
连接：F2 / F4 / F12 / F14。

### F25 · 工具效率与合规
English: Research Tools, Reproducibility & Compliance
目的：保护研究信息、模型与决策审计链。 保持覆盖产出质量、发布流程和数据使用边界。
合成训练：合成：AI生成模型把百万美元当作美元，又将客户押金既加进CFO又算融资流入；一份专家材料标为“未获授权传播”。如何修复，并建立可复现、可发布的研究输出？
评审模型（作答后再读）：先核单位、币种、期间、公式及现金流分类，消除押金重复；与原始报表闭合，保留版本、来源和检查记录。隔离未授权材料，不转发或上传到公共模型/网站，不由摘要绕过授权边界；使用公开可核验替代证据或获取许可。AI、脚本和表格只提升处理效率，不替代责任；按适用机构流程核查保密、利益冲突、研究发布要求及数据许可，现实规则查最新正式文件。所有案例均是教学，不构成法律判断。
迁移：迁移到批量更新25家公司、发布研报或训练网站，设计自动检查、人工复核、失败回滚与来源权限记录。
证据标准：数据、单位、版本和复现链；独立核验、闭合与异常处理；授权、保密与冲突处理；工具提高效率而不代替责任。
